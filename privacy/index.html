Privacy policy

Bible Strong Number app · Effective date: October 2026

This policy describes the Bible Strong Number app. The app does not require an account. It does not collect, sell, or share personal data.

Accounts and personal data

There are no user accounts. The app does not collect personal data, does not sell personal data, and does not share personal data.

The iOS privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) declares Data Not Collected and sets tracking to false.

Analytics and on-device storage

The app does not include analytics SDKs.

Preferences and cache are stored only on the device, using MMKV, AsyncStorage, and localforage. The app never uploads that data.

Scripture and Strong’s

Scripture text and Strong’s data are bundled with the app and used offline.

The operating system and the app store may collect their own telemetry. That collection is outside the app and is governed by the operating system or store, not by this policy.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is not used to profile a child.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page and the effective date above will be revised.
