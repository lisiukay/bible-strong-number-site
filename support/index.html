FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. The app has no accounts.
Does the app work offline?
Yes. Scripture and Strong’s data are bundled with the app.
Is anything I store in the app uploaded?
No. Preferences and cache stay on the device (MMKV, AsyncStorage, and localforage) and are never uploaded. The app does not include analytics SDKs.
Where is the privacy policy?
It is published at the privacy page. Effective date: October 2026.
